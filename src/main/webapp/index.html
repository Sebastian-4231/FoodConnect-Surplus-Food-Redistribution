How It Works

Three Simple Steps to Save Food

Redistributing surplus food has never been simpler. Whether donating, requesting, or delivering, everyone wins.

1

Donors Post Surplus

Donors specify food name, available quantity, pickup location, and subsidized or zero price in seconds.

2

Recipients Request

Community members browse nearby surplus food, customize portions, and set their delivery address.

3

Partners Deliver

Verified delivery volunteers pick up orders from donors and hand them over safely to recipients.
